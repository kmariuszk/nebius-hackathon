Missing Links How it works GitHub ↗ Start a board

A research board that finds the papers your search missed

Give Missing Links a question and a few papers you trust. An agent searches PubMed, preprints and citation links, puts each new paper on a shared board next to your work, and shows the sentence that backs every claim.

Start a board → Watch the 2-min demo
How it works
1
Give it your starting point

A question and the papers you already trust.

2
The agent searches

PubMed, PMC, bioRxiv and journals through Tavily, plus citation links from PubMed, OpenAlex and Semantic Scholar.

3
New papers land on your board

Ranked for your goal and placed next to the papers they connect to.

4
You decide, it keeps going

Keep or discard. It searches around what you keep, and catches you up next time.

Every claim points to the sentence behind it

Tumour-reactive heterotypic CD8 T cell clusters from clinical samples
Ibáñez-Molero et al. · Nature · 2026
“Single-cell RNA-sequencing analysis revealed that T cells from clusters were enriched for gene signatures associated with tumour reactivity and exhaustion.”
✓ In the abstract, word for word · Open on PubMed ↗
  • Checked word for word. Every quote is matched against the source. If it isn’t there, the card says so.
  • One click to the source. PubMed opens with the sentence highlighted.
  • Gaps are shown, not hidden. Each paper lists what the agent couldn’t check.
